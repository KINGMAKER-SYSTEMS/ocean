Fixed the flaky `tests::permission_settings_roundtrip_all_three_modes_and_report_env_mask` in `platform/ocean-os/crates/ocean-daemon/src/main.rs` (effective `SkipAll` where `Manual` was just persisted; passes alone). Root cause is a process-env race with two mutexes guarding the same variables: the settings tests held `YOLO_ENV_LOCK` while every runtime fixture (`fake_convene_state` exporting `OCEAN_CONFIG_DIR`/`OCEAN_MODEL`/`OCEAN_YOLO=1`, restored later by `TestEnvRestore`) held `AUTO_CONVENE_ENV_LOCK`, so a write or restore from one family could land between the target's persist and its effective read; with `OCEAN_CONFIG_DIR` removed the read falls back to the operator's real `~/.config/ocean-rs`, whose legacy `yolo_pref=true` migrates to `SkipAll`, the exact observed value, and the family restored env by hand rather than on drop so a panic polluted later tests. Chose the seam over the lock as the primary fix: `yolo_settings::PermissionPolicyInputs` captures `config_dir` and the raw `OCEAN_YOLO` once per request and owns the policy resolution and both settings bodies; the axum handlers and the free `effective_permission_mode`/`permission_env_override`/`effective_yolo`/`resolve_request_permission_mode` are thin wrappers over `from_process_env()` so production reads and logs are unchanged. The target and six sibling tests resolve against `PermissionPolicyInputs::explicit(tempdir, ...)` with no env, lock or manual restore; one new test, `permission_settings_handlers_resolve_from_process_env`, plus the voice fail-fast test keep the env wiring proven under the lock with drop-time restore. Then unified the two locks into one `crate::tests::PROCESS_ENV_LOCK` (`process_env_guard[_async]`), keeping `AUTO_CONVENE_ENV_LOCK` as a `use` alias for the 180 existing sites and dropping the 65 redundant back-to-back acquisitions that would deadlock a non-reentrant tokio mutex; a post-edit audit shows no function takes both names and every writer of those variables holds the one lock. Daemon `AGENTS.md` gains the test-isolation bullet. Proof with a private target dir: target test 20/20 alone; `cargo test -p ocean-daemon` x5 at default threads and x1 at `--test-threads=16`, all 1066 passed 0 failed (pre-fix control runs at 16 and 32 threads also passed 1065/1065, so the flake is rare locally and the fix removes the race by construction); `cargo fmt --check` clean; `cargo clippy -p ocean-daemon --tests` unchanged at the 4 pre-existing daemon warnings in untouched files.
